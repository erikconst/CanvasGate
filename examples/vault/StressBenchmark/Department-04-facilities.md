The facilities team is reviewing its quarterly plans, owners and delivery milestones. The proposal is to collect feedback from stakeholders, compare alternatives, identify implementation risks, and prepare a short decision record. The first phase covers discovery and interviews. The second phase covers prototypes, cost estimates and vendor comparisons. The final phase covers a limited rollout and lessons learned. Each workstream should record its assumptions and revisit them during the next planning cycle.

#### Facilities decisions
The current planning template requires an owner, a target date, a budget range, an acceptance checklist and links to supporting material. Teams should record unresolved questions before procurement begins. Reports should distinguish experiments from approved commitments. Customer interviews, accessibility reviews, staff training, documentation updates and communications are tracked as separate deliverables. These departmental plans do not amend the authoritative fleet, dispatch policy or current order book.
